# Credits

IXFilm OS exists because several open-source projects did the difficult groundwork first.

## CHDK

The camera loader, Canon IXUS 120 IS port, filesystem support, module system and much of the runtime come from the Canon Hack Development Kit project.

- Project: <https://github.com/CHDK/chdk>
- Licence: GNU General Public License version 2 or later, as stated by the upstream source

IXFilm substantially changes the user-facing behaviour and adds its own resident film-simulation module. It is not an official CHDK build and should not be presented as one.

## Soup Engine

The JPEG decoder, encoder and film-look pipeline are adapted from Soup Engine by its original author and contributors.

- Project: <https://github.com/jiijii0/soup-engine>
- Licence: GNU General Public License version 2

The IXFilm camera build adds memory-saving streaming changes, Canon-compatible JPEG output and integration with the IXUS runtime.

## Film names

The included recipes are original approximations inspired by familiar photographic materials. They are not measured colour profiles and are not endorsed by their manufacturers. All product names and trademarks belong to their owners.
